2M Whites Pharma — field-force management for pharmaceutical distribution teams

About the app

2M is a field-force management application for pharmaceutical distribution teams. Medical representatives, sales representatives, supervisors, area managers and warehouse staff use it to plan and check in to customer visits with GPS verification, manage customer records, capture sales orders and collections, track inventory transfers, and review team performance reports. The interface is available in both Arabic (right-to-left) and English.

Contact us

When reporting a problem, please include your role, the screen you were on, and the app version shown in Settings → About. It helps us reproduce the issue quickly.

Signing in

2M is a business application: your organization issues your account and assigns your role, so there is no self-registration inside the app. Sign in with the phone number and password your administrator gave you.

  • Forgot your password? Contact your organization's system administrator to have it reset.
  • Wrong role or missing screens? Roles are assigned server-side; ask your administrator to correct the assignment, then sign out and sign back in.
  • Interested in using 2M at your company? Email us at the address above.

Common questions

  • Why does the app ask for my location? Only to verify a visit check-in and to record the coordinates of a customer location. Location is requested at that moment, never in the background and never at launch.
  • Why does it ask for contacts access? Only so you can pick a phone number from your address book when adding a customer. Declining leaves the field fully usable — just type the number.
  • How do I switch to Arabic? Settings → Language. The whole interface mirrors to a right-to-left layout.
  • How do I delete my account? Accounts are created and revoked by your organization's administrator. Contact them, or email us and we will route the request.
